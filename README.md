# Exploratory Data Analysis for EV Adoption by State
**SQL Portfolio Project | SQLite | Data Analytics**

## Executive Summary
In this scenario, a fictional transportation research group is trying to understand the growth of EVs per state. This report will be used by a government transportation board to plan future charging infrastructure projects. In order to maximize use of the new infrastructure, the focus will be on the top states that already have a larger EV adoptions, specifically full Electric Vehicles and Plug-In Hybrid Vehicles, as a percentage of total vehicle types. Based on the analysis, the top 5 states that would be good candidates for increase EV charging infrastructure is California, District of Columbia, Hawaii, Washington, and Oregon. Increased infrastructure could lead to greater adoption by these states that have already shown interest. As this data set only covers one-year, next steps would be to gather multiple year data to see if there is a growth trend over time for other states that may not currently have high levels but could grow.
